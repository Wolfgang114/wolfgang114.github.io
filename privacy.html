Privacy Policy

YouTube Bot is a private tool used only by the channel owner to manage his own YouTube channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to read channel and video statistics and to update video metadata on the owner's own channels. It does not collect, sell, or share data about anyone else.

Access tokens are stored privately and used only for this purpose. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube Bot uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
